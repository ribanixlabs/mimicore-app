Install and first start

Is Mimicore safe? Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen)

Mimicore is new and not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer installs for your Windows user only and needs no admin rights. Every update is signed and the app verifies the signature before installing it.

What do I need to run Mimicore? System requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A DirectX 12 graphics card from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel is recommended for live voices; the 28 effects run on any PC. Training your own voice on the PC needs an NVIDIA GTX 16 or RTX 20 series card or newer with 4 GB or more of memory and the driver installed (and at least 10 GB of free disk space). No NVIDIA card? Train free in the cloud instead.

Does Mimicore work on Mac, Linux, Android or iPhone?

No. Mimicore is for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only right now.

How big is the download and how much space does it need?

The installer is about 20 MB. On first start you download one voice-engine quality tier once: Fast about 1.1 GB, Balanced about 1.3 GB, Ultra about 1.8 GB. Optional add-ons: Trained voices 0.3 GB, Voice Training 2.5 GB (NVIDIA only), Video dubbing about 3.2 GB, the voice gallery 27 MB.

Which quality tier should I choose: Fast, Balanced or Ultra?

Balanced (about 1.3 GB) is the recommended choice for most PCs. Fast (1.1 GB) has the lowest delay and graphics memory use, best for gaming and low-spec PCs. Ultra (1.8 GB) has the most polished speech and files but is about 3 times slower. Every tier speaks 23 languages and you can add or remove tiers any time in Settings > Add-ons.

Does Mimicore need admin rights or an internet connection?

No admin rights: it installs for your Windows user only. The live voice, effects and speech run on your PC, but you need internet to sign in, to download models, and to check your plan. If you go offline, your plan stays valid for up to 7 days, and free live minutes you use offline are sent the next time you are online.

Do I have to sign in to use Mimicore?

Yes. Sign in with Google, Discord or an email code (Settings > Account, or when the app asks). It is free: the Free plan gives 20 live minutes a day.

How do I sign in? The sign-in does not finish or the code email does not arrive

In Mimicore choose Settings > Account > Sign in, pick Google, Discord or email, and confirm on the website page that opens; the app then signs in by itself. If you use email, the 6-digit code can take a minute: check spam, and ask for a new code if it is older than a few minutes. If a sign-in link seems stuck, close the website tab, press Sign in in the app again and use the new page. Still stuck? Press Talk to a person and we open a ticket.

How do I update Mimicore?

Mimicore checks for updates when it starts and every few hours, downloads them in the background and asks you to restart. You can also check yourself: Settings > About > Updates > Check for updates. Updates are signed and verified before installing.

What is an early build (beta) and how do I get one?

If you reported a problem through Support, you get an email with a link to an early build containing the fix, so you can test it before everyone else. It is never installed automatically: open Settings > About > Updates > Check for early release. Afterwards press It is fixed or Still broken on your ticket in the Support tab.

How do I uninstall Mimicore? Are my voices deleted?

Uninstall like any app: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Mimicore > Uninstall. Uninstalling does not delete your data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore), so your voices and settings stay. Delete that folder yourself to remove everything.

Where does Mimicore keep my files, voices and logs?

In %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore. Voices are in the voices folder, generated speech in outputs, logs in logs (mimicore.log), settings in settings.json. Open it from Settings > Privacy & data > Open data folder.

Can I move Mimicore to another PC?

Install Mimicore on the new PC and sign in. Copy the voices folder from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore\voices on the old PC to the same place on the new one to bring your voices. Studio works on up to 2 PCs per account (see Studio PC limit).

The virtual microphone (Discord, Zoom, OBS, games)

How do I use Mimicore in Discord, Zoom, Teams, OBS or a game? Set up the virtual microphone

Open Live > Output > Set up virtual mic. Mimicore downloads the free VB-CABLE driver from vb-audio.com, checks its signature and installs it (click Yes when Windows asks; restart if Windows asks). Then in the other app choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable) as the microphone. Discord: User Settings > Voice & Video > Input Device. OBS: add an Audio Input Capture source and select CABLE Output. Zoom, Teams, Meet: Settings > Audio > Microphone. Games: set the in-game voice input device, or set CABLE Output as the Windows default recording device.

Discord: people hear me strangely, or Krisp noise suppression ruins the voice

In Discord User Settings > Voice & Video turn off Discord's own Noise Suppression (Krisp) and Echo Cancellation. They fight the changed voice. Keep Mimicore's Noise suppression on instead.

Others cannot hear me / my friends hear nothing

Check that the voice is started (the big Start button shows Live), that Live > Output > Virtual mic is not Off, and that the other app's microphone is CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable), not your normal microphone. If the cable does not show in the other app, restart Windows after the VB-CABLE install and open Mimicore again.

The virtual microphone / CABLE Output does not appear in other apps

Restart Windows after the VB-CABLE install, then open Mimicore again. Restart the other app too (Discord, OBS and games only list audio devices when they start). If it still does not appear, run Live > Output > Set up virtual mic again.

Set up virtual mic fails or the VB-CABLE install does not finish

Click Yes on the Windows permission prompt, close other audio apps, and try again. If Windows asks for a restart, restart and press Set up virtual mic once more. Mimicore downloads the VB-CABLE package from vb-audio.com and checks its signature; a blocked download (a firewall, VPN or antivirus) is the usual cause. If it keeps failing, press Report a bug so we can see the log.

I hear an echo or howling

Use headphones, or turn off Hear myself on the Live page. With speakers, the microphone picks up its own output.

How do I hear my own changed voice?

Turn on Hear myself on the Live page (Output section) and pick your headphones as the output. Use headphones so the microphone does not pick up the speakers.

Live voice changer

How do I start the live voice changer?

On Live, pick a voice or effect from the list and press the big Start button, then speak. The status line shows the delay, the processing time per block and any glitches. Press Start again (or use the start/stop hotkey) to stop.

The voice sounds robotic, glitchy or cuts out

Close other programs that use the graphics card (games, video editors, browsers with video), choose Low quality on instant voices or the Fast tier in Settings > Add-ons, and check Settings > General > Processing uses your best graphics card. If the status says Struggling, your PC cannot keep up with the current quality.

There is too much delay (latency) or lag

Use Low quality on instant voices (about 0.35 s); trained voices have the lowest delay (about 0.3 s on a mid-range GPU and they are auto-tuned to your PC). Bluetooth headsets add their own delay, so use wired headphones. Close other graphics-heavy programs.

What do Low, Medium and High quality mean on the Live page?

They are for instant voices. Low (about 0.35 s delay) reacts fastest, good for gaming. Medium (about 0.45 s) is recommended. High (about 0.5 s) sounds most natural, for streams and recordings. Trained voices are auto-tuned instead.

My microphone is too quiet or too loud

Keep Automatic level on (quiet microphones get louder), or raise the mic level in Windows sound settings. If it is too loud or clipping, move back a little or lower the level in Windows. Calibrate to my voice shows warnings for quiet or clipping microphones.

Keyboard, fan or background noise comes through

Turn on Noise suppression and set the Noise gate to Medium (Off, Light, Medium or Strong). The noise gate silences everything between your words.

How do I pick the right microphone?

On Live, open the Microphone section and choose your microphone from the list (the default is Windows default). Press Refresh after plugging in a new one. The level meter moves while the voice is running. If you unplug a microphone, Mimicore waits for it or falls back to the default microphone.

What is Mute, and what is the Original-voice test (bypass)?

Mute silences your microphone without stopping the voice, so unmuting is instant. Original-voice test (bypass) passes your real voice through unchanged so you can check levels.

What is Voice tuning: pitch and voice strength?

For trained voices only. Pitch shifts in semitones (plus 12 is one octave up). Voice strength sets how strongly the sound is pulled toward the trained voice. Both apply instantly.

What does Calibrate to my voice do?

For trained voices. Read one sentence and Mimicore measures your pitch and your room, then sets the pitch and noise gate for the closest match. The settings are saved for that voice and load when you pick it.

What does "Struggling" mean in the status line?

Your PC cannot keep up with the live voice. Close other programs that use the graphics card, choose Low quality, or switch to the Fast tier.

Can I add an effect on top of my cloned voice?

Yes. Use Live > Add an effect and pick Radio, Cave, Robot or any of the 28 effects; it is layered on top of your voice.

I get "Microphone disconnected" or "Speakers or virtual mic disconnected"

Mimicore reopens an unplugged microphone, speaker or virtual cable by itself and shows a notice while it waits. Plug the device back in or choose another one; if the default microphone is available the live voice continues with it.

The voice engine had a problem and my own voice is passing through

After an error in the voice engine Mimicore passes your own voice through so the call is not silent. Stop and start the live voice to reset it. If it happens again, press Report a bug in Support.

Voices: instant, gallery, mix, train, import

How do I create a voice of my own (instant voice cloning)?

Voices & effects > + Instant voice, give it a name, choose whose voice it is (your own, or someone who gave you permission), record about 15 to 20 seconds of natural speech in a quiet room (or import a clean recording), then Create voice. It is ready in under a minute and works live, in Speak and in Dub. Background noise is removed automatically. Instant voice creation needs the Studio plan (or a Studio trial).

Instant voice says I need Studio or "part of Studio"

Cloning, mixing, importing and training voices, long Speak and Dub clips, and your own voices live are part of the Studio plan. You can start a 7-day Studio free trial on your account page (a refundable small payment-method check; billing starts after the trial; cancel any time before).

What do I need to record a good voice sample?

About 15 to 20 seconds of natural, continuous speech in a quiet room, close to the microphone, not too loud. If the recording is too quiet Mimicore says so; if it clips, move back a little. If there is lots of background noise Mimicore removes it, but a quieter room sounds better.

Why does Mimicore ask for a consent statement?

When the voice belongs to someone else, they must record a short spoken consent statement and Mimicore checks that it is the same person. This is part of our rule: only your own voice or voices you have permission to use.

What is the voice gallery?

19 original ready-made voices, each a blend of two speakers so none copies a real person. In Voices & effects click Get the voice gallery (27 MB) once, press the play button to hear a voice and Add to my voices to use it. Gallery voices are free to use.

What does Mix voices do?

It blends two of your instant voices into a brand-new voice. Move the slider between A and B and press Create mix. Trained (RVC) voices cannot be mixed yet. Mixing is part of Studio.

How do I train a voice for the closest likeness?

Training copies a voice from 10 to 30 minutes of clean recordings of one person. On this PC (NVIDIA card needed): install the Voice Training add-on, add recordings, choose a quality and press Start training (Quick test about 10 to 20 minutes, Good about 25 to 50 minutes, Best about 1 to 2 hours). Without NVIDIA: choose Cloud (free), which trains on a free Google Colab GPU in your own Google account with the same trainer and quality. Training is part of Studio.

How long does voice training take? How long until my trained voice is ready?

Training time depends on the quality you choose: Quick test about 10 to 20 minutes, Good (recommended) about 25 to 50 minutes, Best about 1 to 2 hours on your own NVIDIA card. In the cloud (free Google Colab GPU) it takes longer: about 25 to 40 minutes, 50 to 90 minutes and 1.5 to 3 hours. Keep Mimicore (or the Colab tab) open while it trains.

Training is greyed out or says I need an NVIDIA graphics card

Training on the PC needs an NVIDIA graphics card (GTX 16 or RTX 20 series or newer, 4 GB or more) with its driver installed, and at least 10 GB of free disk space on the drive. Use Cloud (free) instead on any other PC.

Train a voice screen switches to Cloud on its own (fixed in 2.6.0)

Fixed in version 2.6.0: a PC with a usable NVIDIA card now stays on This PC. Update Mimicore (Settings > About > Updates). If you are on 2.6.0 or later and it still happens, press Report a bug.

How does free cloud training work, and is it private?

Voices & effects > Train a voice > Cloud (free): Connect Google Drive and allow access (Mimicore can see only the files it creates), add recordings, choose a quality and press Upload and open Colab. In the Colab tab press the play button, click Run anyway and allow Drive access. Keep the Colab tab open: Mimicore shows progress and installs the voice automatically. Your recordings go only to your own Google Drive and Colab, never to our servers, and are moved to your Drive trash afterwards unless you chose to keep them.

Colab says no GPU is available, or the Colab training stopped

Free Google GPUs are limited at busy times. Try again later; training continues from the last checkpoint. Keep the Colab tab open while it trains. You can restart a stopped training by starting again with the same name.

How do I import an existing RVC voice?

Voices & effects > Import RVC voices, choose the folder containing the voice (.pth or .onnx and an .index file). Only import voices you have the rights to use. Importing is part of Studio.

How do I rename or delete a voice, or turn a recording into a voice (convert a file)?

On the voice card: Rename, Delete (stop the live voice first), Preview to hear a sample, Convert a file to turn a recording into that voice.

Can I use a voice live without the Studio plan?

Built-in voices and gallery voices work live on Free (20 minutes a day) and Live (unlimited). Using your own voices live needs Studio.

Effects and soundboard

What voice effects are there?

28 effects such as Robot, Demon, Chipmunk, Radio, Cave, Cathedral, 8-bit, Giant, Ghost, Elf, Dark Lord, Stadium announcer and Underwater. They run instantly on the CPU and are free with no time limit. Press the play button to hear one and Use to pick it for Live; Apply to a file processes a recording.

How does the soundboard work?

Soundboard has built-in sounds (air horn, applause, drum roll, sad trombone and more). Add sounds brings in your own MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC or M4A clips up to 5 minutes, as many as you like. Click a tile to play it into the virtual microphone; turn on Also play on my speakers to hear it yourself. Give each sound a hotkey, set volumes in Edit and drag tiles to reorder. Sounds play even when the live voice is off.

Soundboard sounds are not heard in Discord

Turn on Play into the virtual mic on the Soundboard page and make sure the other app uses CABLE Output as its microphone.

Speak (text to speech) and Dub

How do I make Mimicore speak text in my voice (text to speech)?

Open Speak, type or paste text (or load a .txt file), pick a voice and a language, adjust Emotion and Variation if you like, then choose Speak now (plays on your speakers and/or the virtual microphone, so people in your call hear it, and a copy is saved) or Save to file (renders a WAV).

Which languages does Speak support?

23 languages: Arabic, Danish, German, Greek, English, Spanish, Finnish, French, Hebrew, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Dutch, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Swedish, Swahili, Turkish and Chinese.

Speak is limited to 450 characters or 30 seconds

On the Free plan Speak, Dub and file conversion clips are limited to about 30 seconds (Speak about 450 characters). Studio has no limit.

Speak sounds slow to start or is not in my chosen voice

Playback starts after the first sentence (about 1 second); long texts are rendered sentence by sentence. Trained voices speak through the default voice, converted with the pitch matched automatically. The Ultra tier is the most polished for files but slower.

Where are the generated speech files saved?

In %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore\outputs, or where you choose with Save to file. Generated files are marked as AI-generated in their metadata.

How does Dub a video work?

Install the free Dubbing add-on once (Settings > Add-ons, about 3.2 GB). Open Dub a video, choose a file (MP4, MOV, MKV, M4A, MP3 or WAV), the spoken language (or Detect automatically), the target language and a voice, set Original audio, and press Dub it. Mimicore transcribes, translates sentence by sentence, speaks each line in your voice and fits it to the original timing, fully offline. You get a WAV audio track and an SRT subtitle file to add in any video editor.

Dub a video says it needs the Dubbing add-on

Install it in Settings > Add-ons (Video dubbing, about 3.2 GB), then return to Dub a video. It is free.

Can Mimicore put the dubbed audio on the video for me?

Not yet: it creates a WAV track and an SRT subtitle file. Put the audio on the video in any editor (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, Clipchamp) and add the subtitles if you like.

Hotkeys

How do I set hotkeys and push-to-talk?

Settings > Hotkeys: click a box, then press a key or combination. Available actions: start/stop the live voice, push-to-talk (hold), mute/unmute, original voice on/off, next/previous voice or effect, switch straight to a voice, and stop all soundboard sounds. Hotkeys work while a game has focus. Use combinations (Ctrl + Alt + key) or keys you do not use elsewhere (F7 to F12, numpad): a bound key is reserved for Mimicore while it runs.

A hotkey does not work or conflicts with another app

Another app may already use that key, or the key is reserved. Pick a different combination such as Ctrl + Alt + a letter. Hotkey problems are listed in Settings > Hotkeys.

I paid but my plan has not changed

Sign in on the website account page, type the code in the box under Choose your plan and press Redeem a free code. A gift code gives Live or Studio for a number of days or for life, and works once per account. Discount codes (percent off) are different: they apply when you pick a plan at checkout.

My code says "not valid", "already used" or "expired"

Check for typing mistakes (codes are not case sensitive). "You already redeemed this code" means this account used it. "Already been used up" means the code reached its number of uses. "Expired" means its redeem-by date passed. "This is a discount code" means use it at checkout when you choose a plan instead.

What is the Studio two-PC limit and how do I move to a new PC?

Studio works on up to 2 PCs per account. If you see "This PC isn't linked to your Studio plan", open Settings > Account > Your PCs (or the website account page), remove a PC you no longer use, then press Refresh. A removed PC cannot be used with that account again. Free and Live have no PC limit.

I used my free live minutes. When do they refill?

Free live minutes refill every day. Live or Studio removes the limit. Effects and the soundboard never use free minutes.

I started a trial before and cannot start another

There is one free trial per person and per PC. After the trial you can still subscribe, or use the Free plan. If you think this is a mistake, press Talk to a person.

Account and privacy

Is my voice uploaded to your servers? Is Mimicore private?

No. Live voice, cloning, effects, speech, dubbing and on-PC training all run on your PC. Microphone audio is processed in memory and never stored or sent. We store only your account email, plan and payment records. Optional cloud training uses only your own Google Drive and Colab, never our servers. Error reports are off unless you turn them on, and a support ticket sends only what you approve.

What data do you store about me?

Your email address, sign-in method, plan and subscription status, free minutes used today, payment records (payment id, plan, amount, time), and for Studio the list of your PCs. We never receive your audio, voices, recordings or the text you type. Details: the privacy policy on the website.

How do I export or delete my account and my data?

In Mimicore: Settings > Privacy & data > Export my account data, or Delete my account. The same is on the website account page. Cancel any active subscription first. Deleting removes your plan, sign-in, tickets, chats and logs; payment records stay for accounting without a link to your account. Voices and files on your PC are not affected.

What are error reports and should I turn them on?

Settings > Privacy & data > Send error reports (off by default). When on, a crash sends a short technical report (error message, app version, Windows version, a random installation id). Never audio, voices, text, your email or your account. Kept for 90 days. It helps us fix bugs.

What does Copy support info contain?

Settings > Privacy & data > Copy support info copies versions, graphics card, key settings and the last log lines, with your Windows user name and email addresses removed. Paste it into an email. In Support you can instead press Report a bug and approve the log that gets attached.

Can I clone any voice, such as a celebrity?

Only your own voice, or a voice whose owner gave you permission. Mimicore records a spoken consent statement and marks generated files as AI-generated. Impersonation, fraud and deception are forbidden by the Acceptable Use Policy.

What is the age limit?

Mimicore is for people 18 or older.

Support, tickets and contact

How do I contact a person? Where is the support email?

Press Talk to a person in the Support tab and I will create a ticket for you; you get an email at every step. The website contact page lists the support email for billing, refunds and security reports.

How do I report a bug?

In Support, describe what you did and roughly when, then press Report a bug. Mimicore picks the matching part of its local log, removes your Windows user name and email addresses, and shows you what it would attach; nothing is sent until you press Send ticket with log (or Send without log). You can follow the ticket in Your tickets and get emails at every step.

What happens after I report a bug?

You get an email right away. Our automatic engineers analyse it: if it is not a defect you receive an explanation by email; if it is a real defect it is confirmed to you, a fix is built and tested, and you get an email with an early build to test. Press It is fixed or Still broken on your ticket. A person steps in if the automatic fix cannot solve it.

How long does a fix take?

It can take some time because every fix is tested before it reaches anyone. You get an email at each step, and your ticket's status is always shown in Support under Your tickets. I cannot promise a date.

The app crashed or will not start

Restart Mimicore. If it keeps failing, open Support and press Report a bug (the log from the crash is attached with your approval), or copy support info from Settings if the app opens. If a new version fails to start three times, Mimicore rolls back to the previous release by itself and does not offer that version again.

Error messages

"Sign in to use Mimicore"

Mimicore needs you to be signed in (it is free). Open Settings > Account > Sign in.

"You've used today's free live minutes"

The Free plan has 20 live minutes a day; they refill tomorrow. Live or Studio removes the limit.

"... is part of Studio" or "... is limited to 30 seconds on your plan"

That feature (cloning, mixing, importing, training, your own voices live, long clips) needs the Studio plan. See Settings > Account; you can start a 7-day free trial there.

"Install the Dubbing add-on first" or "Install the Trained voices add-on first"

Install the add-on in Settings > Add-ons: Video dubbing for Dub a video, Trained voices (RVC v2) for trained or imported voices.

"Nowhere to play: set up the virtual mic"

Speak needs somewhere to play: turn on My speakers, or set up the virtual mic in Live > Output and turn on the Virtual microphone output.

"Not enough voice in the recording"

The recording had too little speech. Read the sentence aloud (about 5 to 12 seconds for calibration, 15 to 20 seconds for a voice sample) closer to the microphone and try again.

"Your microphone is quiet" or "Your microphone is clipping"

Quiet: speak closer or raise the input level in Windows sound settings. Clipping: move back a little or lower the level.

"Stop the live voice first" or "A training is already running" or "Another job is still running"

Mimicore does one heavy job at a time. Stop the live voice (or wait for the current training or job to finish, or press Stop), then try again.

"Training needs at least 10 GB of free disk space"

Free up space on the drive where Mimicore keeps its data (%LOCALAPPDATA%), then start the training again.

"The mixer blends instant voices. Trained (RVC) voices can't be mixed yet"

Mix voices works with instant voices only. Trained or imported RVC voices cannot be mixed yet.

"That file is larger than 2 MB"

That file is too large for this field. Use a smaller recording or text file.

More questions

Does Mimicore work with Valorant, Fortnite, CS2, VRChat, Roblox, Minecraft, Skype, WhatsApp, Telegram, Twitch or TikTok Live?

Mimicore works with any PC app or game that lets you choose a microphone: set CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable) as the microphone or input device in that app, or set it as the Windows default recording device. It does not touch the game itself. It does not work for phone calls on a phone.

Will I get banned in a game for using a voice changer?

Mimicore does not modify or inject into any game: it only provides a virtual microphone. Whether a game or server allows voice changers is up to that game's rules, so check them. Never use a voice to deceive, harass or impersonate anyone: see the Acceptable Use Policy.

Can I make my voice sound like a woman, a man, a child or another gender?

Use a gallery voice (19 original voices, free), an effect such as Chipmunk or Giant, or create an instant or trained voice of a person who gave you permission. Trained voices also have a Pitch control (semitones). Mimicore does not change a voice's gender by a single switch.

Is there a limit on how long I can talk live?

On the Free plan, 20 live minutes per day with built-in or gallery voices (it refills daily). Live and Studio plans have no live limit.

Can I use Mimicore for phone calls?

Mimicore changes the voice on your PC, so it works in calling apps on your PC (Zoom, Teams, Discord, Skype, WhatsApp Desktop) with CABLE Output as the microphone. It does not work on your phone or for normal phone-network calls.

Does Mimicore use a lot of my graphics card or CPU?

More error messages and known limits

"Windows is blocking microphone access"

Windows privacy settings stop desktop apps from using the microphone. Open Windows Settings > Privacy and security > Microphone, turn on "Let desktop apps access your microphone" (and the main "Microphone access" switch), then press Start again in Mimicore. This is a Windows setting; Mimicore cannot change it for you.

"Microphone ... is not connected" or "Output ... is not connected"

The device you picked in Live is not plugged in or has been renamed. Pick another device in the Microphone or Output list (Windows default always works), press Refresh, and start again.

"No speakers or headphones found" or "Nothing to output"

Mimicore needs somewhere to send the changed voice: switch on Hear myself (choose your speakers or headphones) or pick the virtual microphone (CABLE Input) under Output.

"Audio did not start (timed out)"

The audio devices did not answer in time. Close other apps that hold the microphone or speakers (a call, a recorder), choose the Windows default devices, and press Start again. If it happens again, press Report a bug in Support.

"Not enough disk space: the Training Pack needs about ... GB free"

Free up space on the drive where Mimicore keeps its data (C: by default), or use Cloud (free) training instead, then install the Voice Training add-on again.

Google Drive errors: "Google access expired or was removed" or "Google Drive access was not allowed"

Open Settings > Add-ons > Cloud training and press Disconnect, then connect Google Drive again and tick the Google Drive box when Google asks. Mimicore only needs the permission to see the files it creates.

"The trained voice has not arrived in Drive yet"

The cloud training is finishing. Wait a minute and try again; Mimicore installs the voice automatically when it appears.

"The recording is ... s long. At least ... s of speech is needed" or "The consent statement has almost no speech"

Record longer, clearer speech: 15 to 20 seconds for an instant voice, and read the consent sentence aloud for the consent statement. Quiet rooms work best.

The dubbed audio sounds literal or the translation is odd

Dubbing translates sentence by sentence and idioms can come out literal, and short lines can be merged. Use clear speech in the original video, short sentences, and check the SRT subtitle file before you publish.
