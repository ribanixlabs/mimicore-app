AI voice changer for Discord, games and streams

Mimicore changes your voice live on Windows and sends it to Discord, Zoom, OBS and your games through a virtual microphone. It runs on your own PC, with about a third of a second of delay on a mid-range GPU.

See plans

Use Mimicore as a Discord voice changer in four steps

  1. 1

    Install Mimicore, then open Live › Output › Set up virtual mic. It installs the free VB-CABLE driver for you.

  2. 2

    In Discord, open User Settings › Voice & Video and set Input Device to CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable). Turn off Discord's Noise Suppression and Echo Cancellation.

  3. 3

    In Mimicore, pick a voice or effect and press Start. Use Hear myself with headphones to check how you sound.

  4. 4

    Set a push-to-talk key and a start/stop hotkey so you can switch voices without leaving your game.

Questions

Does Mimicore work with Discord?

Yes. Mimicore creates a virtual microphone called CABLE Output. Select it as your input device in Discord's Voice & Video settings and turn off Discord's own noise suppression and echo cancellation, because they fight the changed voice.

Is it a real-time voice changer?

Yes. Depending on the quality setting and your PC, the delay is roughly 0.3 to 0.5 seconds for typical settings. A Lite setting adapts to slower PCs and integrated graphics.

Do I need a powerful graphics card?

No. Mimicore tunes itself to your PC and picks a model size that suits it. Slower PCs start at a lighter but still good setting, and the 28 effects work right away.

Is it free?

There is a free plan with 20 live minutes a day and 28 free effects. You can start a 7-day free trial of the paid plans from your account page.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

No. Mimicore is built for Windows 10 and Windows 11.
